Bridal FAQ

How far in advance should I book my bridal package?+
We recommend booking 3-6 months before your wedding date, especially during peak wedding season (November-February). This allows time for consultations, trials, and pre-bridal skincare treatments.
Do you provide on-location bridal services?+
Yes! For all bridal packages, we offer on-location services at your home, hotel, or wedding venue. Our team arrives with complete professional setup including lighting, mirrors, and premium products.
Can I customize my bridal package?+
Absolutely! All our packages are fully customizable. During your consultation, we'll work with you to create a personalized bridal beauty plan that fits your needs, preferences, and budget.
What brands do you use for bridal makeup?+
We use only premium, internationally recognized brands including MAC, Charlotte Tilbury, Dior, Estée Lauder, Bobbi Brown, and more. All products are cruelty-free and suitable for sensitive skin.
Do you offer trial sessions before the wedding?+
Yes! All bridal packages include at least one trial session. During the trial, you'll experience your complete bridal look including makeup, hair, and styling. This ensures you're 100% happy with your wedding day look.
What is your payment policy for bridal packages?+
We require a 50% advance payment to confirm your booking, with the remaining balance due on or before the wedding day. We accept cards, UPI, net banking, and offer EMI options for packages above ₹50,000.
