About Glow & Glam

Our Luxury Story

Glow & Glam Luxury Salon was born from a passion for beauty and a vision to create a sanctuary where luxury meets artistry. Founded in 2011 by renowned beauty entrepreneur Meera Kapoor, our salon has grown from a single studio to a multi-location luxury brand.

Our mission is simple: to make every client feel like royalty. We combine time-honored beauty traditions with cutting-edge techniques and premium products to deliver results that speak for themselves.

Every treatment at Glow & Glam is a carefully curated experience, designed to rejuvenate, transform, and inspire. From our award-winning makeup artists to our certified skincare specialists, every member of our team shares an unwavering commitment to excellence.
